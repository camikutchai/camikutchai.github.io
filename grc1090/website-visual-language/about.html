We’re a café made for slowing down and enjoying the little things. Whether you’re stopping in before class, meeting a friend, or taking a break in the middle of a busy day, we want you to feel comfortable here. Our goal is to create a welcoming place where you can settle in, enjoy something delicious, and leave feeling a little more refreshed.

Our menu is inspired by the fun of trying something new. We serve classic espresso drinks alongside creative lattes, smooth matcha, and refreshing teas. You can stick with a familiar favorite or explore flavors like cardamom and rose, strawberry matcha, Mango Sticky Rice matcha, or a fruity hibiscus refresher. There’s something for different moods, seasons, and cravings.

We also love pairing drinks with a sweet treat. Our bakery menu features playful flavors and eye-catching pastries, from ube crinkle cookies and pistachio cruffins to Dubai chocolate brownies and mango-passionfruit mochi donuts. Each item brings its own flavor and personality, making it easy to find a new favorite—or choose something you already love.

More than anything, we want our café to feel like a place you can make your own. Come by for a quick drink, bring a friend to catch up, or stay awhile and enjoy a quiet moment. We’re here to make your visit feel warm, relaxed, and worth coming back for. We’re glad you found us.

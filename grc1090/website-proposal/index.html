Introduction

SLO Start

SLO Start is a local coffee shop in San Luis Obispo designed to give customers the perfect start to their day. It offers fresh, unique coffee in a welcoming and relaxed environment.

Target audience

SLO Start's target audience is college students, young adults, and local coffee lovers who enjoy trying new and unique drinks. The shop especially appelas to people who like creative latte flavors, seasonal specials, and a fun, inviting atmosphere to study, socialize, or start their day.

Our goals:

  • Order drinks and food quickly and easily.
  • Explore unique latte flavors.
  • Customize drinks based on flavor, milk, sweetness, and other preferences.
  • Create a welcoming expericne where customers can study, socialize, or relax.
